[LIGHTLY AMUSED] First, airlines may ask for volunteers to take a later flight in exchange for compensation.

In the United States, the Department of Transportation requires airlines to seek volunteers before involuntarily denying boarding because of an oversale. Exact rules vary by country.

This is why you sometimes hear the offer climb: two hundred dollars... four hundred... and suddenly somebody remembers they never liked this destination anyway.
