# U1-L02 — Understand an Introduction

## Teaching goal
Help an Arabic-speaking A1 adult listener identify the person in a fresh spoken introduction and capture at least two important facts without translating every word.

The lesson must make one listening strategy visually memorable:

1. First listen: **Who is the person?**
2. Second listen: collect **2–3 fact slots**.
3. Use the pronoun to decide **who the fact belongs to**.

The core contrast is first person vs third person:

- `I'm / I live` → information about the speaker.
- `He's / He lives / She's / She lives` → information about the person being introduced.

Do not turn this into a grammar lecture. It is a listening-comprehension lesson.

## Teacher / voice
One warm Egyptian English teacher speaking directly to one Arabic-speaking adult beginner. Natural Egyptian Arabic for explanation. Clean General American English for every English span. Observant, reassuring, focused, lightly playful.

Bracketed performance directions and pronunciation markup are silent instructions, never spoken. Preserve the authored teaching meaning and protected English pronunciation markup.

## Visual concept
A polished modern English-learning studio built around a **voice-message / listening detective** motif.

Use a persistent visual system that the learner can understand at a glance:

- a voice-message card / waveform;
- a person identity chip;
- four fact slots: **Person · From · Lives · Role**;
- first-person and third-person lanes that are clearly distinct;
- animated fact capture as the teacher extracts Alex's information;
- a clean ownership handoff when the lesson switches to Omar and Layla.

The visuals should feel adult, modern, calm and premium — not childish flashcards.

## Suggested semantic scenes
The exact scene timing must be derived from final audio, not guessed from transcript part boundaries.

1. **Listen first** — introduce the situation and play Alex's full voice-message model. Do not reveal all facts before the model finishes.
2. **Find the person** — isolate `I'm Alex.` and establish the first listening question: “مين الشخص؟”.
3. **Capture the facts** — fill From / Lives / Role one by one, then show the four-fact Alex summary.
4. **Who does the fact belong to?** — visually contrast `I'm from Canada` with `This is Omar / He's... / He lives...`.
5. **Third-person ownership** — Omar facts stay attached to Omar; then a short Layla example reinforces `She's`.
6. **Listening strategy close** — compact three-step strategy card and CTA to the post-video listening activity.
