# Creative direction — Al Amaar / Orchid Elegance

Create a premium English-language brand-product film for **Al Amaar HPL & Wood Solutions**, focused on **Orchid Elegance — DG 818 A-208 PR+**.

## Brand feeling

Architectural, refined, tactile, quietly confident. The film should feel like a premium materials campaign from a high-end interiors brand — not a catalog slideshow and not a loud social-media ad.

Use a restrained palette inspired by the product photography: charcoal/near-black, warm ivory, sand, muted bronze, and natural walnut. Typography should be elegant, spacious, modern, and editorial. Motion should be slow, deliberate, and cinematic: controlled push-ins, masked image reveals, subtle parallax, fine rules, and restrained kinetic type.

## Product story

Lead with texture and atmosphere before specifications. Show the product in context, then let the technical benefits support the emotional promise.

Product facts sourced from the live Al Amaar product page:
- Product: Orchid Elegance
- Code: DG 818 A-208 PR+
- Product type: HPL
- Category / collection: Cane
- Application: Interior
- Surface: Textured HPL
- Sheet formats: 8 × 4 / 10 × 4
- Thickness options: 0.8 mm / 1.0 mm / 1.2 mm
- Listed benefits: scratch resistant, easy to clean, durable surface, fade resistant, heat resistant
- Ideal for: kitchens, wall cladding, furniture, offices, retail spaces, hospitality

Do not introduce unsupported performance claims beyond the product page.

## Visual source images

Use the product photography from the Al Amaar website. `scripts/prepare-assets.sh` downloads the exact source URLs into the render workspace. Treat them as the hero visual material; do not replace them with generic stock.

## Editorial structure

1. **Texture / hook** — darkness, macro texture, minimal line: “Some surfaces fill a space. Others define it.”
2. **Product reveal** — full application image; introduce ORCHID ELEGANCE and code.
3. **Material character** — close-up / panel image, tactile detail, “Textured HPL · Interior”.
4. **Performance** — benefits appear as restrained editorial labels, never infographic-heavy.
5. **Applications** — elegant interior photography with concise applications.
6. **Brand close** — product code, sample-oriented CTA, then “Al Amaar — Surfaces that shape the space.”

## Audio / pacing

One English narrator. Warm, refined, measured, premium, calm. Avoid an overexcited commercial read. Allow tasteful pauses and let visuals breathe around the narration.

No paid music generation. Soundtrack remains disabled for this first premium product test; the visual edit and voice should carry the film.

## QA priorities

Premium typography, image crop quality, no stretched product photography, no clipping, generous safe margins, clean line breaks, no dense spec cards, and meaningful late-scene holds. Every scene should remain elegant when frozen at the QA final-hold frame.
