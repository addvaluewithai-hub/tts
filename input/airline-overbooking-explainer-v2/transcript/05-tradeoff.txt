[REVEALING] So why risk paying people at all?

Because airlines are balancing two costs.

Cost one: a seat leaves empty and can never be sold again.

Cost two: too many people show up and the airline pays to fix the problem.

The goal is not, "sell as many tickets as possible." It's: sell just enough extra tickets that the expected empty-seat loss is smaller than the expected overbooking cost.
